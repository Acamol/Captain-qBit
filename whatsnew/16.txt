New: a Pieces tab in the torrent details. It shows every piece of the torrent as a block — green for downloaded, yellow for downloading, grey for missing — with the piece count and size above it. While the tab is open and the torrent is still downloading, the map refreshes every few seconds. Torrents with a very large number of pieces are grouped so the map still fits on screen.

The torrent detail tabs now scroll sideways, so the five of them fit without their names wrapping onto two lines.

New: Russian translation, thanks to Andrei Stepanov.

Crash reports now include the heap, native heap and thread count, which helps track down out-of-memory crashes.
